# Lending process description (revision 3)

## 1 Purpose

This document describes how the library lends media to its members.

## 2 Loans

### 2.1 Lending

A member may borrow at most five media at the same time. The loan period is
28 days. A loan can be extended twice, unless another member has reserved the medium.

### 2.2 Returns and fees

Media returned after the due date cost a fee of 0.50 EUR per day and medium.
The fee is capped at 10 EUR per medium.

## 3 Reminders

Three days before the due date the member receives a reminder by e-mail.

## 4 Accounts

Members can only see their own loans. Staff accounts see all loans.
